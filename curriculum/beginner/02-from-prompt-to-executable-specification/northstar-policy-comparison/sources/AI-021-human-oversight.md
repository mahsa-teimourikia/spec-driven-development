# AI-021 — Human oversight for underwriting assistance

**Owner:** Director of Underwriting Risk
**Authority:** Mandatory domain policy
**Revision:** `ai-021-v4-training`

Commercial-policy comparisons used to influence a coverage, pricing, or eligibility decision require review by an authorized underwriter. The system must retain a reviewer receipt linked to the comparison before the result enters the underwriting decision record.

Exploratory comparisons may be displayed with a non-decision label, but may not be promoted to a consequential record without review.

This is fictional course material, not a real organizational policy.
