# OBS-008 — Safe AI trace metadata

**Owner:** Observability Platform
**Authority:** Platform standard
**Revision:** `obs-008-v3-training`

AI feature traces record request, requirement, evidence, model-route, and timing identifiers. They must not store policy text, retrieved passages, raw prompts, or personal data. Exceptions require approval from both the privacy and observability owners.
