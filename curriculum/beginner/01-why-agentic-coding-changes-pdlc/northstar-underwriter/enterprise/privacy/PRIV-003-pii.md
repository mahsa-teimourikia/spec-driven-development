# PRIV-003 — Confidential data in AI systems

Owner: Privacy Office
Version: 3.2
Effective: 2026-07-01

Confidential or restricted customer data SHALL only be sent through enterprise-approved model routes. Any exception requires a documented decision by the CISO delegate, defined scope, compensating controls, an expiry date, and revocation conditions.

Machine-readable control: `C-02` in `context/organization/enterprise-controls.json`.
