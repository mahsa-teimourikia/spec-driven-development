# AI-021 guidance — Oversight by risk tier

Owner: AI Governance
Version: 1.3
Effective: 2026-06-01

- Tier 3: human approval is required before an external action or consequential recommendation.
- Tier 2: human review must be available before the result is relied upon.
- Tier 1: post-hoc monitoring is permitted.

JIRA-4821 is classified high risk in the authoritative change context and therefore uses the Tier 3 interpretation. Machine-readable control: `D-01` in `context/domain/underwriting-controls.json`.
