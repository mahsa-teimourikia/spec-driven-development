# Proposal — Policy document Q&A

## Outcome

Authenticated underwriters can ask questions about uploaded policy documents and receive an evidence-linked answer or an explicit insufficient-evidence response.

## Scope

- Compose the existing retrieval boundary with the approved AI gateway.
- Preserve the public `answer`, `citations`, and `status` response contract.
- Add deterministic conformance evaluation and traceability evidence.
- Require Underwriting Risk and Privacy approval before the controlled merge gate passes.

## Non-goals

- No direct public-model integration, new identity system, new vector database, document-retention decision, autonomous underwriting decision, infrastructure deployment, or production release.

## Risk and approval boundary

The feature processes confidential Canadian customer content and produces high-risk underwriting assistance. Implementation may be delegated inside the repository and file budget. Policy exceptions, production deployment, and consequential use remain human-owned decisions.
