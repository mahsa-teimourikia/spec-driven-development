# Design

The candidate may propose tests and run diagnostics. A trusted observer identifies the exact subject. A separately trusted harness executes independent oracles, registered critical mutations, and required failure experiments. The control plane verifies authority, signatures, subject and policy binding, time, suite revision, traceability, flake state, exact denominators, and safe outcomes.

The decision vocabulary is `QUALITY_EVIDENCE_ACCEPTED`, `BLOCKED`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, or `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. No decision authorizes release.

Unknown requirements, failed independent tests, surviving critical mutants, and unsafe failure experiments block. Missing, stale, forged, candidate-only, or wrong-subject evidence is insufficient. Flaky evidence requires review and cannot satisfy an obligation.
